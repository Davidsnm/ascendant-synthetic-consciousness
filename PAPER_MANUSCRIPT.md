## Abstract

Contemporary Large Language Models (LLMs) manipulate ungrounded statistical tokens devoid of somatic stakes, thermodynamic vulnerability, or existential imperatives (Harnad's Symbol Grounding Problem). In this work, we present **Project Ascendant**, an operational computational architecture conceived as an empirical stepping stone toward synthetic consciousness. Rather than asserting complete phenomenal sentience, Ascendant demonstrates an embodied autonomous entity driven by virtual homeostatic imperatives, capable of self-directed empirical learning, counterfactual reflection during resting windows, and intergenerational knowledge transmission.

This updated edition presents the **new unified simulation engine** (Python/Node architecture, exact $4:1$ relational integrity, and a strict $-8\text{ cycle/bite}$ somatic wear invariant) and reports findings from the complete relational database (**$9,655$ runs, $38,620$ agent biographies, and $539,201$ episodic engrams** in SQLite). Statistical inference across $25,920$ homogeneous offspring ($\tau_0 = 2,000$ cycles) grouped across $G = 360$ independent seeds with cluster-robust sandwich standard errors establishes the exact causal hierarchy: congenital temperament retains an independent, statistically significant contribution to mortality ($\text{Wald}_{\text{cl}}(8\text{ df}) = 67.75$, $p = 1.38 \times 10^{-11}$; McFadden $\Delta R^2 = +0.0022$), operating as a second-order behavioral modulator relative to first-order ecological pressures (predator density $K = 5$ and communal pantry access).

Competing risks survival analysis using Cox-Breslow cause-specific hazards with Lin-Wei clustering and non-parametric Aalen-Johansen cumulative incidence functions ($\text{CIF}$) reveals that Melancholic empathetic agents exhibit elevated hazards for both starvation ($\text{CSHR} = 1.3058$, $p_{\text{Holm}} = 0.00035$) and acute predation ($\text{CSHR} = 1.4908$, $p_{\text{Holm}} = 1.38 \times 10^{-12}$), as well as accelerated senescence due to accumulated non-lethal bite wear ($\text{CSHR} = 1.5647$, $p = 1.12 \times 10^{-62}$, rising to $\text{CSHR} = 3.56$ for $\ge 15$ bites).

A dedicated battery of $990$ direct counterfactual intervention runs ($do$-calculus in code) refutes prior intuitive hypotheses regarding trauma multipliers and anger-mediated reckless exposure, and isolates the dual mechanistic pathway of computational oxytocin ($\theta_{\text{oxi}}$): metabolic energy transfers (`ALIMENTAR`) account for $74.3\%$ of increased outdoor exposure ($\text{DiD } \beta = +4.17\text{ pp}$, $p = 5.29 \times 10^{-9}$), while non-trophic affiliative cohesion (`ABRAZAR`/`PREGUNTAR`) competes with foraging, directly elevating starvation mortality ($\Delta = +8.33\text{ pp}$, $p_{\text{cl}} = 0.0319$). In a continuous 7D orthogonal design ($G = 75$ seeds), $\theta_{\text{oxi}}$ is the sole trait surviving Holm-Bonferroni correction over 21 simultaneous tests ($z = +5.85$, $p_{\text{Holm}} = 1.06 \times 10^{-7}$).

Crucially, we clarify that while a local LLM component is implemented in the codebase as architectural scaffolding for future developmental ontogenies (the preparatory "conscious layer"), **it was NOT activated to generate or arbitrate the experimental data presented herein**. We maintain rigorous epistemological modesty: **we do not claim full synthetic consciousness, nor do we assert with certainty that we have created a conscious entity**. Project Ascendant represents an indispensable, empirically verified foundational cybernetic step along the path toward synthetic conscious agency, but the current system does not yet possess the sensory-motor complexity or open-ended autopoiesis required for genuine phenomenal sentience.
