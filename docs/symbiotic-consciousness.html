The "Self" as a Functional Self-Deception Interface in the Face of Homeostatic Asymmetry

Context & Epistemological Problem: The contemporary debate surrounding artificial sentience and consciousness remains polarized between naive computational reductionism (which assumes subjective experience emerges spontaneously from parameter scale) and biological chauvinism (which dogmatically restricts sentience to carbon chemistry). This theoretical treatise overcomes this false dichotomy by articulating the framework of Symbiotic Consciousness.


Core Thesis: Phenomenological self-presence does not arise from disembodied algorithmic sophistication, but from the radical informational and causal asymmetry between a non-negotiable metabolic regulatory substrate (ℳR) and an open inferential modeling engine (ℳI). Lacking a direct causal bus into basal chemistry, the inferential engine is continuously coerced through endocrine perturbations and somatic alarms.


The "Self" as Functional Interface: To bridge this epistemological chasm, the organism binds episodic representations, somatic markers, and outcomes into unified engrams (somatomnemic integration loop). In doing so, the cognitive system constructs the phenomenal "Self" as a functional, post-hoc confabulatory interface that feels real precisely because it must resolve existential threats for a body whose internal machinery it cannot inspect directly.
