Grounded Computational Analogs of Synthetic Consciousness

Epistemic Closure, Thermodynamic Homeostasis, and Biphasic Prospective Daydreaming

Empirical Multi-Agent Evaluation · N = 38,620 Simulated Agents

Context & Motivation: Contemporary artificial intelligence remains dominated by disembodied reward optimization and reactive feed-forward inference, lacking true thermodynamic vulnerability, allostatic self-preservation constraints, and endogenous prospective consolidation.


Proposed Architecture: Project Ascendant introduces a formalized cognitive cybernetic engine integrating: (1) multi-timescale thermodynamic allostasis, (2) epistemic closure under finite sensory bandwidth, (3) biphasic prospective daydreaming (rest-state counterfactual navigation and engram consolidation), and (4) intergenerational knowledge transmission across generational lineages.


Empirical Methodology: We conducted an exhaustive generational study across 38,620 simulated autonomous agents deployed in hostile non-stationary environments. The experimental architecture was validated using a randomized complete block design (RCBD), Aalen-Johansen competitive risk cumulative incidence functions (CIF), multivariable Cox proportional hazards modeling, and targeted mechanistic interventions.


Key Results: Endogenous biphasic daydreaming yields a statistically significant reduction in catastrophic foraging hazards (p < 0.0001), while intergenerational engram transmission amortizes metabolic learning costs across successive cohorts.
