Context & Motivation: Contemporary AI remains dominated by disembodied reward optimization and reactive feed-forward inference, lacking true thermodynamic vulnerability, allostatic self-preservation constraints, and endogenous prospective consolidation.


Proposed Architecture: Project Ascendant introduces a formalized cognitive cybernetic engine integrating: (1) multi-timescale thermodynamic allostasis, (2) epistemic closure under finite sensory bandwidth, (3) biphasic prospective daydreaming, and (4) intergenerational knowledge transmission across generational lineages.


Empirical Methodology: We conducted an exhaustive generational study across 38,620 simulated autonomous agents validated using randomized complete block design (RCBD), Aalen-Johansen CIF, multivariable Cox regression, and targeted mechanistic interventions.
